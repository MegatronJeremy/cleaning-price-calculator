How the maths works

Cost per labour hour = pay × (1 + payroll %) + supplies per hour + monthly overhead ÷ labour hours sold. Labour hours for the job = sq ft ÷ sq ft per labour hour + bathrooms × extra hours per bathroom (sample: 0.25 standard, 0.5 deep, 0.6 move-out) + add-on hours. Break-even = labour hours × cost per labour hour + miles × cost per mile. Quote = (labour hours × cost per labour hour ÷ (1 − margin) + travel) × (1 − discount), rounded up to the next $5.

Example with the numbers above: cost per labour hour is $24.64 + $2.50 + $3.75 = $30.89. A 2,000 sq ft standard clean with 2 bathrooms is 4.5 labour hours (2.25 hours on site for a team of 2). Break-even is $147.41 and the quote is $185.

Labour hours are person-hours: two cleaners working 2 hours is 4 labour hours. Recurring discounts come straight out of your margin: at a 20% target margin, a 15% weekly discount leaves only about 6% (before rounding and travel).
